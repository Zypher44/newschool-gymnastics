YOUR VOICE MATTERS

Ask questions. Share a win. Get support.

It is okay to ask for help, say something feels wrong or ask for a practice adjustment. You do not need to earn a badge or complete a challenge to speak up.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted adult now. App messages and these links are not an emergency service.

Everyday practice conversations

Athletes can ask their coaches about a skill or learning goal. Parents can contact their coaches or gym director about the program. Athlete messages need a selected observer: an approved linked parent or a second coach. All three people see the new thread. If no suitable observer is available, arrange an open, observable conversation with responsible adults through your gym.

Gym membership and a staff role do not verify a person's age, screening or training. Your gym is responsible for checking these and applying its communication policy.

Worried about someone's behaviour?

If a concern involves a coach, director, parent or another adult, you can seek help outside that person's reporting line. You do not have to use an app conversation or get their permission to open the resources below.

Ordinary app messages are visible to their selected participants. They are not an independent or anonymous reporting service. Do not send an allegation into a shared thread if a participant may be involved.

  • Gymnastics Ontario — Safe Sport resources and reporting guidance
  • Sport Integrity Canada — reporting and questions about the Canadian Safe Sport Program

The Canadian Safe Sport Program has a defined scope; it does not automatically cover every local-club matter. Its reporting page explains who is covered and how to ask for direction if unsure. A provincial or club process may apply. A club director is not an independent reporting body.

This page does not submit a report or alert anyone. Follow the appropriate external process. Speak to a trusted adult who is not involved if that helps you access support.

Practices can be fun without pressure

Practice Passport stamps celebrate coach-approved learning, teamwork and personal progress. Breaks do not reset stamps. Check-in answers, journal entries and safety reports are not used to calculate passport eligibility.

Choose goals with your coach. Stop and ask for support if something hurts or feels unsafe. Never add repetitions just to finish a game target.

Guidance: Coaching Association of Canada — Rule of Two. These application guardrails support transparent communication; they are not a Safe Sport certification or a substitute for gym policies, trained adults and appropriate reporting procedures.
