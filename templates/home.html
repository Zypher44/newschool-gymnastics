FROM CHECK-IN TO FOLLOW-UP

Give the next conversation some context.

A check-in can start a useful conversation. Coaches can review what an athlete shares and keep notes alongside their training history.

  1. 01

    The athlete checks in

    Athletes report how they feel, including energy, sleep, soreness and whether they want to talk to their coach.

  2. 02

    The coach reviews

    Coaching priorities bring check-in concerns into view, so the coach can decide what needs a conversation before training.

  3. 03

    The coach records context

    Add a coach note on the athlete profile. Return to their check-ins, skills, testing and feedback as training continues.

Check-ins support communication and record keeping. They do not diagnose conditions, predict injuries or replace a coach's judgment or medical care.

MAKE PRACTICE AN ADVENTURE

Celebrate learning, teamwork and personal progress.

Practice Passports turn coach-approved achievements into collectible stamps. Optional practice quests encourage athletes to ask questions, encourage teammates and reflect on what they learn.

🙋 Ask & Learn

Encourage skill questions and clearer conversations with the coaching team.

🤝 Great Teammate

Recognize encouragement and helpful teamwork through coach-awarded stamps.

🏅 A personal passport

Follow each athlete's own milestones. Breaks do not reset stamps; wellness answers and safety concerns are not scored.

Goals stay within the coach's agreed practice plan. Athletes should never add repetitions or hide pain to finish a game target.

KEEP THE CONVERSATION OPEN

A clear route for every voice.

Athletes message coaches with an explicitly selected approved parent or second coach included. Parents can contact their coaches and directors; coaches can contact directors. Participants can see who is included in each thread.

For a concern about someone's behaviour, families can use independent support and reporting resources without sending an app message first. These communication guardrails support Safe Sport practices; the app is not an independent reporting service or a compliance certification.

Who is NewSchool for?

Gymnastics directors and coaches who want to bring athlete check-ins, skill development and family communication into a shared workspace.

Does it replace registration and billing?

NewSchool focuses on coaching and athlete development. Keep your existing registration and payment tools; automated integrations are not promised.

What happens after I request a demo?

Your enquiry is saved for the NewSchool team to review and follow up using the email you provide. No account or payment is needed to send the request. A demo time is arranged separately.

What does it cost? Can we discuss a pilot?

Pricing and pilot terms are discussed directly and are not published as fixed plans yet. Ask about pricing or a pilot for your gym. Sending an enquiry does not start a paid subscription.

What does setup involve?

A director creates the gym, adds training groups and manages the roster. Coaches, athletes and parents request membership; the director approves access. Parent-to-athlete links also need approval. Discuss setup assistance, data entry and any import needs before starting; automatic migration is not promised.
